# ⚔️ Shadow Monarch: Arise

Defeat mythological monsters, extract their shadows, and build an army that follows you forever. A 2D browser game: no install, no dependencies.


## Features

- **30 monsters from world mythology** across six regions (Greek, Norse, Celtic & British, African, Asian & Middle East, American).
- **Three monster types**: goblins (melee), mages (ranged magic) and flying dragons, each in a small/fast and a big/strong version.
- **Kings and courts**: big monsters are 👑 kings with an army of guards. Clear the guards to reach the king.
- **Shadow extraction**: after every kill, add the fallen monster to your army. Duplicates level up.
- **Persistent army**: your shadows are saved in the browser.
- **Three worlds** reached through a gate hub.
- **Desktop and mobile** support, with pause, mute, fullscreen and a graphics toggle.

## How to play

1. Start at **The Gates**. Walk to a gate and press **↑** or **Enter** (or tap the gate) to enter a world.
2. Fight the monster. You are **immortal**: you can get hurt but never die, and mana is infinite.
3. When the monster falls, press **X** (or Enter, or tap) to **extract its shadow**.
4. Pick your next prey from the monster board and repeat.

Press **G** to return to the gates at any time.

### Touch

Turn your phone sideways. Buttons appear on the game screen: movement and jump on the left, powers on the right. Use **🎮 Touch buttons** to show or hide them and **⛶ Fullscreen** for the best view.

## Powers

- **Dagger strike**: three-hit combo (slash, reverse slash, spin kick).
- **Shadow Step**: a quick dash.
- **Telekinesis (E)**: lifts the enemy high for about 1.2 s, then slams it down for heavy damage, a stun, and a wipe of nearby guards. Press **E** again to throw it early. Range about 320 px, recharge 4 s.
- **Shadow Clone (C)**: two clones run to the enemy and strike in 3-hit combos. Enemies attack them as decoys. Each vanishes after 2 hits or 10 s. Recharge 6 s.
- **Rasengan (R)**: charge a sphere (about 0.5 s), then throw it. It homes in and explodes on the first guard or monster it touches for big damage and a stun. With a clone alive, charging is twice as fast and the hit is 40% stronger. Recharge about 3 s.
- **Arise (A)**: opens the summon board to call one of your extracted shadows. With no army, it summons 3 temporary soldiers.
- **Dragon's Fear (W)**: stuns the enemy.
- **Monarch's Domain (S)**: full-power state with doubled damage for a few seconds.

Your shadows keep the type and size of their monster, and they never die: a fallen shadow returns into you and regenerates.
