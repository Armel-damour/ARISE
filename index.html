How to play

New powers: C Shadow Clone Technique: two blue clones pop up next to you (recharge 6 s), run to the enemy and strike in 3-hit combos. Enemies chase and attack the clones as decoys; each clone vanishes after 2 hits or 10 s. R Rasengan: your hero charges a spinning sphere in his hand (about 0.5 s, interrupted if you get hurt), then throws it: the sphere leaves his hand, homes in on the enemy and explodes on the first guard or monster it touches (big damage, knockback and a stun; it fizzles out after 1.5 s if it misses). If a clone is alive it helps: charging is twice as fast and the hit is 40% stronger (the clone is used up). Recharge about 3 s.

Power: E Telekinesis. Your hero raises a hand, lifts the enemy high into the air and holds it there (about 1.2 s); press E again to throw it down early, or wait and it slams on its own. The slam does heavy damage (more against big monsters), stuns the enemy and wipes out nearby guards. Range about 320 px, recharge 4 s. Shadows keep hitting the lifted enemy.

Phone: turn it sideways; the buttons appear on the game screen (left: move and jump, right: powers). Computer: use the keyboard keys listed below.

Also new: the game now starts at The Gates. Walk to a gate and press ↑ / Enter (or tap it) to enter a world: Shadow Realm (original), Frost Realm (snow and ice, you slide when you run) or Ember Realm (lava geysers erupt: dodge them, they also burn monsters and shadows). Press G during a fight or on the monster board to return to the gates.

←/→ move · ↑ jump · ↓ (or J) dagger strike (tap 3 times for a combo: slash, reverse slash, spin kick) · D Shadow Step (dash, 10 MP) · A Arise: opens the summon board so you can pick which extracted shadow to call (small ones cost 12 MP, big ones 25 MP); with no army it summons 3 temporary soldiers (40 MP) · W Dragon’s Fear: stun (30 MP) · S Monarch’s Domain: full power (100 MP) · P or Esc pause · Stop returns to the title screen. X Extract: after every kill the game pauses until you add the fallen enemy to your army. You are immortal: you can get hurt but never die, only your shadows can. Monsters come as goblins (melee), mages (ranged magic) and flying dragons (fire and dives), in small fast and big strong versions. Your extracted shadows keep their type and size. Mana is infinite. 30 monsters from world mythology: after every extraction pick your next prey from the region tabs. Big monsters are 👑 kings and fight with their own army of guards: kill the guards near you to reach the king. Your shadows never die: if one falls it returns into Sung Jin Woo and regenerates, and your army is saved. Press Enter or tap the game to begin.
